Setup Guide

RP2350 - Uses internal pull-up resistors. Buttons wire to GND.

  1. Step 1: Download Bundle

    Download the all-in-one bundle (.zip) containing CircuitPython firmware and synth files.

    Download Bundle (.zip)

  2. Step 2: Flash CircuitPython

    Hold BOOTSEL on your Pico 2 while plugging in USB. Drag circuitpython-pico2.uf2 from the bundle onto the "RPI-RP2" drive. A new "CIRCUITPY" drive appears.

  3. Step 3: Copy Synth Files

    Open the CIRCUITPY-contents folder from the bundle. Drag all files (code.py, config.json, lib/, wav/) onto the CIRCUITPY drive. The synth starts playing!

  4. Step 4: Configure

    Build your hardware layout above, then click Connect and Save to Device. No file copying needed after this; config is saved to flash.

    Alternative: Download code.py from the code panel and copy it to CIRCUITPY manually.

Wiring Reference

Input Pins Wiring
Button GP0-GP22 Switch between GPIO and GND (internal pull-up, no resistor needed)
Touch (GPIO) GP0-GP22 Conductive pad (copper tape, foil, wire) connected to GPIO only (capacitive sensing via touchio)
Touch (MPR121) I2C GP16/GP17 MPR121 breakout via I2C; up to 12 touch channels per board
Pot / LDR GP26, GP27, GP28 Pot: outer legs to 3V3 + GND, wiper to ADC pin. LDR: voltage divider with 10k resistor.
Accelerometer I2C GP16/GP17 LIS3DH or MPU6050 module; SDA/SCL to GP16/GP17, VCC to 3V3
Audio Out GP15 PWM audio; connect to speaker/amp via RC filter

Wiring Guide

Buttons

Connect momentary push buttons between a GPIO pin and GND. Internal pull-up resistors are enabled in software (RP2350), so no external resistors are needed.

Connect momentary push buttons between a GPIO pin and GND. Internal pull-up resistors are enabled in software (RP2040), so no external resistors are needed.

    GP0-GP7 ----[ Button ]---- GND
    GP0-GP7 ----[ Button ]---- GND

Potentiometers

Connect 10kΩ pots with outer legs to 3V3 and GND, wiper to GP26/GP27/GP28.

    3V3 ----[ Pot ]---- GP26/27/28 ----[ Pot ]---- GND

LDR

Voltage divider with LDR and 10kΩ resistor. LDR to 3V3, junction to ADC pin, resistor to GND.

MPR121 Capacitive Touch

I2C on GP16 (SDA) / GP17 (SCL). 12 touch channels per board. Address 0x5A default.

Accelerometer (LIS3DH)

I2C on GP16/GP17. Provides X, Y, Z acceleration for tilt and motion control.

OLED Display (SSD1306)

128x64 I2C display on GP16/GP17. Address 0x3C.

Speaker (PWM Audio)

PWM on GP15 through RC low-pass filter (1kΩ + 100nF) to speaker.

Pin Summary

  Buttons: GP0-GP7 (8 max) | Analog: GP26-28 (3 max)
  I2C: GP16/17 (shared)    | Audio: GP15    | LED: GP25
